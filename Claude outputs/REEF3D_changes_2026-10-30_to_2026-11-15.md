# REEF3D: additions and improvements, 30 October – 15 November 2026

Branch `hans_dev`, local repository `~/Codelite/REEF3D`.

## Overview

- 568 commits (510 without merges). All but two are by Hans Bihs; the two others are L.-Carlos Dempwolff's PRs #182 and #183.
- 3,728 files changed: about 68k lines in `src`.
- 617 new regression cases (1,635 in total now): about 252 CFD, 130 SFLOW, 118 NHFLOW, 62 FNPF and 17 HYDRO.
- New validation suites in `Validation/rheology` and `Validation/sediment`.
- `release-notes.txt` got 46 new entries. They cover mainly the semi-implicit solvers, rheology, soil and sediment. Much of the rest of this summary comes from the code and the new defaults in `ini.cpp`.

## NHFLOW

- **Semi-implicit step rebuilt:**
  - Selection is now A 510 11/12/13; A 534 1 is retired.
  - A 510 11 is a new one-step predictor after Basilisk, using BCG advection. It now also runs on curvilinear, multi-block and refined grids.
  - With A 505 1 (the new default), the nonlinear terms are second order in time.
  - The step uses Popinet's (2020) multilayer flux, which fixed the wrong plateau the Stoker dam break converged to as dt went to zero.
- **Non-hydrostatic pressure in the semi-implicit step (A 520 > 0):**
  - A coupled eta–phi system after Basilisk: band LU for 1D, FGMRES with a column multigrid for 2D and MPI.
  - The matrix is assembled directly; the preconditioner runs in single precision; new keys A 506 (tolerance) and A 507 (split solve, optional).
- **Semi-implicit features:**
  - Breaking A 550 1/3/4, plus a new A 550 5 that adds the deep-water breakers of FNPF.
  - Non-hydrostatic pressure on wet/dry grids, with an iterated wet pattern.
  - Optional limiter on the vertical velocity, A 558.
- **Tracers, density and stratification (A 600–A 627):**
  - Salinity and temperature, with a linear or UNESCO equation of state.
  - Baroclinic pressure gradient after Shchepetkin and McWilliams (2003).
  - Mixing damped by the Richardson number, and k-epsilon with a buoyancy term.
- **Semi-Lagrangian momentum advection,** A 549 1.
- **Memory and speed:**
  - Lazy allocation, a "field diet" for unused options and a compact layout of the work arrays.
  - A direct tridiagonal solve in 1D.
  - Typical gains are 20–40 % in memory and time, with results bitwise identical.
- **Other:**
  - Bed velocity boundary condition A 518, and a new default for bed friction A 519.
  - Bed roughness map B 58 (`ks-map.dat`).
  - Fixes to VRANS layers, bodies, the hull and thin body (X 15 11 added mass), and dry land next to deep water (A 541).

## SFLOW

- **Semi-implicit step:**
  - Selection is now A 210 11/12/13; A 226 and A 229 are retired.
  - A 210 11 is a one-step BCG predictor and is the recommended scheme.
  - A 210 12/13 are second order in time.
  - It uses the multilayer face depth and has fourth-order horizontal operators on uniform grids.
  - Automatic time step when A 228 is not given.
- **Semi-implicit capabilities:**
  - Coupled non-hydrostatic pressure on curvilinear grids and with mesh refinement.
  - Ship pressure X 10 3 on refined grids, and the towed body X 10 2.
  - Correct wall terms under wind and Coriolis forcing.
- **Horizontal diffusion in flux form,** so it exerts no net force. This gives better breaking for Ting & Kirby and Mase & Kirby.
- **Wave breaking A 246 4,** the Kennedy/Barthelemy model of NHFLOW. Dry land as a wall: A 221 2.
- **New mesh-refinement modules:** hydro, non-hydrostatic, turbulence and wave (`sflow_amr_*`).
- **Coupling to the new HYDRO models** (rain, infiltration, catchment; see the last section).

## Rheology (new)

- **Rheology core:** Herschel-Bulkley, Herschel-Bulkley-Coulomb, mu(I) and Coulomb-viscoplastic laws, plus depth-integrated basal closures (Coulomb, Voellmy, Pouliquen-Forterre and others).
- **SFLOW,** W 120–129: single-layer rheology, including pore pressure and the semi-implicit step.
- **CFD,** W 130–134:
  - Comes with the dynamic diffusion form D 24, which fixed a 92 % speed error of a Herschel-Bulkley film under air.
  - Fixed the cross terms of the 3D implicit diffusion.
  - W 110 2–7 are retired.
- **Validation:** cases 02–10 in `Validation/rheology`.

## CFD

- **Time stepping:**
  - Unsplit Godunov one-step scheme (BCG). It runs about 3× faster than N 40 3 at the same time step.
  - Gravity-wave time step limit N 54.
  - Air-band time step N 57/58, plus the related keys N 59, N 63 and N 65.
- **Free surface:**
  - Single-phase level set F 41 1, and F 41 2 with closed adiabatic air pockets.
  - Narrow band F 48.
  - Fast-sweeping initialisation of the signed distance.
  - Fixes to PLIC.
- **PIC/FLIP free surface (`pic_f*`):** F 76–88, with APIC and the ghost fluid method.
- **Solids:**
  - Cut cells D 1 2, and projection faces closed inside solids D 32.
  - Wall-law diffusion.
  - Fixes to the immersed boundary method and direct forcing.
  - Floating bodies with the semi-implicit step.
  - Stabilised added mass X 14 11 / X 197, and fluid-momentum coupling X 198.
- **Mesh refinement (reefamr, about 48 commits):**
  - Air pockets, implicit air and closed solids.
  - Static boxes G 16, one interface width on all levels G 28, a hydrostatic restriction G 29.
  - Load-balanced patches G 40, a composite preconditioner cycle G 48, AMR for nets, CPM regions Q 85/86.
- **Soil stress model, S 200–217:** Biot u–p consolidation of the seabed after Titi Sui, with liquefaction potential and `REEF3D_CFD_Soil` output.
- **REEFMG:** Metal GPU device on Apple Silicon (N 16, N 17), and coarse-grid agglomeration N 15.

## FNPF

- **Theta-Robin time integration A 310 5 (about 38 commits):**
  - 2D and 3D, MPI, wetting–drying, floating bodies and mesh refinement.
  - Time step from an accuracy target (N 52, N 53, N 55).
  - New keys A 302–A 308.
- **Breaking A 350 5:** deep-water breakers following Romero et al. (2012) and Derakhti et al. (2018), keys A 367 and A 404–A 412.
- **Explicit steps:** RK3 and RK4 share building blocks, and Laplace stage solves are warm-started (A 321, A 328, A 430).
- **Coastline:** coastline viscosity, wet-only clamps and a fast-sweeping coastline (A 332–A 358).
- **Wave generation:** wave ramp B 101/102, consistent relaxation zones B 95, and a self-consistent cnoidal wave.

## Sediment, scour, CPM

- **Scour gauges,** P 83–89 and P 187–199, for FNPF and NHFLOW:
  - Sumer-Fredsøe incremental scour, a sediment-balance scour, and scour-protection parameters.
- **Sediment transport:**
  - Sediment inflow S 129/130.
  - Hindered settling S 127.
  - Nielsen phase lead and inertia bed shear S 37 / S 140.
  - Limits for thin water S 131–133, and a morphological factor ramp S 105.
  - Fixed the bed load at open edges.
  - Hydraulic roughness separate from grain roughness, S 28 2.
- **CPM:**
  - Graded sediment with hiding/exposure factors (Wu, Wang & Jia, 2000).
  - Implicit Coulomb friction of the packed bed and a dilation limit Q 88/89.
  - Diagnostics Q 87, and CPM with mesh refinement.
- **DEM lift** E 32–34 (Saffman-Mei, Magnus).
- **Validation:** van Rijn trench and LIP11D.

## Bodies, fish, CORAL, SEASTATE, HYDRO

- **Fish (about 30 commits):**
  - Deforming bodies X 195 with an inextensible midline and recoil correction.
  - Gait control X 199 and a lateral line X 194.
  - A brain X 192/193 and X 200–203, with an optional external brain over TCP (`tools/fish_brain/brain_server.py`).
- **STL remeshing,** X 184–190.
- **CORAL:**
  - New power take-off modules: hydraulic PTO, generator and WEC control (schedule or user library).
  - Vortex-induced vibration of mooring lines.
  - Unsteady aerodynamics.
- **SEASTATE:**
  - Online collocation against observations, A 787–789.
  - GSE closure A 776–778, the Hersbach-Janssen limiter A 769, and an initial-spectrum box.
- **HYDRO (new):**
  - Rain, evaporation and infiltration (constant or Green-Ampt), and rasters, R 10–R 50.
  - HBV-type catchment model with kinematic-wave routing, reservoirs and hot start, R 100–110.
  - Coupling to SFLOW.
- **LAGOON output:** nets, membranes and curvilinear grids.
