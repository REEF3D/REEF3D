# 32 NTHMP benchmark 5: subaerial granular slide on a 45° slope (Viroulet et al. 2014)

The laboratory benchmark 5 of the NTHMP landslide workshop (Galveston 2017): dry glass beads in a triangular cavity behind
a vertical gate on a 45° slope, the front face of the material at the water line; the gate is lowered and the beads run
into the water. IRPHE flume (Ecole Centrale Marseille), 2.2 m long, 0.2 m wide, four wave gauges. 1D x-z with the slide
layer (W 150) under SFLOW and NHFLOW.

## Data

`bm5_case1_d1.5mm.txt`, `bm5_case2_d10mm.txt`: t (s), η WG1-WG4 (cm), every 0.01 s (the records `test_1mm5.txt`,
`test_10mm.txt` of the benchmark are every 0.005 s), t = 0 at the release. From the benchmark page
`www1.udel.edu/kirby/landslide/problems/benchmark_5.html`.

## Set-up

| | case 1 | case 2 |
|---|---|---|
| beads D | 1.5 mm | 10 mm |
| water depth H | 0.148 m | 0.150 m |
| triangle L (= front face height B) | 0.11 m | 0.135 m |

- Slope 45° from the left wall (x = 0) to the toe at x = 0.30 + H; the gate (the front face of the material) at x = 0.30 m,
  where the slope meets the water line; the material between the slope and z = H + L for x < 0.30 (`W 151`).
- Gauges WG1-WG4 at 0.45 / 0.75 / 1.05 / 1.35 m from the gate (sketch of the benchmark), the end wall 1.9 m from the gate
  (2.2 m flume; from the arrival of the wave reflected at it at WG4).
- Bulk density 1500 kg/m³ (volume fraction 0.6 of the beads, 2500 kg/m³, dry; the benchmark's value; the water filling
  the pores is not modelled), Coulomb 24° (`W 120 1`, `W 121 24`), interface drag `W 155 0.02`.
- dx 2.5 mm (880 cells), 2.5 s; NHFLOW 10 layers.

| case dir | water | keys |
|---|---|---|
| caseN_sflow_h | SFLOW, explicit HLL, hydrostatic | A 210 3, A 220 0 |
| caseN_sflow_nh | SFLOW, quadratic non-hydrostatic pressure | A 220 2, W 162 0.1 |
| caseN_nhflow_nh | NHFLOW, explicit, non-hydrostatic, 10 layers | A 10 5, A 510 2, A 520 2, W 162 0.02 |

```bash
cd Validation/rheology
for c in case1_sflow_h case1_sflow_nh case1_nhflow_nh; do tools/run_case.sh <REEF3D> <DiveMESH> 32_slide_nthmp_bm5/cases/$c <run>/bm5_$c; done
python3 tools/bm4_eval.py --bench 5 --h 0.148 --align crest --name 32_bm5_case1 32_slide_nthmp_bm5/bm5_case1_d1.5mm.txt <out> \
        "SFLOW hydrostatic"=<run>/bm5_case1_sflow_h "SFLOW NH, W 162 0.1"=<run>/bm5_case1_sflow_nh "NHFLOW NH, 10 layers"=<run>/bm5_case1_nhflow_nh
```

(case 2: `--h 0.15 --name 32_bm5_case2` and `bm5_case2_d10mm.txt`). Results and discussion in the README of
Validation/rheology (section 32).
