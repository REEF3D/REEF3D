# CORAL: hydro/

Engineering hydrodynamic loads (no resolved fluid needed).

## Planned content

* hydrostatics, linear and on the instantaneous wetted surface
* hydrodynamic database: added mass A(ω), damping B(ω), excitation, drift coefficients, QTFs
* Cummins equation: radiation convolution and state-space approximation
* first-order wave excitation and second-order drift (Newman, full QTF)
* Morison loads with relative motion, MacCamy–Fuchs correction, marine growth
* slamming and breaking-wave impact models
* nonlinear Froude–Krylov loads from the incident wave field (REEF3D FNPF/NHFLOW or analytical)
* incident wave kinematics: REEF3D's wave theories when running without a fluid grid
